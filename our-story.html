20+Years Of Home Care

How We Began

Built around one coordinator's promise

Stackly started with a simple frustration: home health care in India was either impersonal agencies or overworked, informal help with no accountability. Our founder, a nurse herself, believed families deserved something in between — trained care with a human face, delivered right at home.

Today every senior we look after is paired with a coordinator who knows their routine, their favourite chair, and their family by name. That one-to-one relationship is still the whole model, even as we've grown to five cities.

Meera KrishnanHead Home Care Nurse & Co-Founder

Our Journey

Milestones along the way

A quick look at how a single home-visit service grew into Stackly.

2006

First home visits in Coimbatore

Meera began offering nursing and companionship visits to seniors recovering at home, with a notebook and a scooter.

2012

In-home therapy programmes launch

We introduced structured physiotherapy and recreational activities delivered directly inside each senior's own home.

2017

Medical partnerships formalised

We brought on our first geriatric physician and built structured post-hospitalisation home care plans.

2021

Expansion to Chennai and Bengaluru

Demand from families with parents in different cities pushed us to open two more home care coordinator teams.

2026

1,000+ homes and counting

Five cities, fifty coordinators, and the same rule we started with: one coordinator, one family, always reachable.
